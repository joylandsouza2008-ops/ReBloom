Privacy Notice

ReBloom is a free student project by Team Phoenix Queens, built for the Sahyadri Science Talent Hunt 2026. It helps mothers return to work after a career break. This notice explains, in plain language, what personal data ReBloom uses, why, and the choices you have under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act). For your data, we are the Data Fiduciary.

1. The personal data we use

DataWhere it comes fromWhy we use it
Name, email address, profile photo and account IDYour Google account, when you sign inTo create your account, show your name and photo, and keep your profile private to you

We only ask for what these purposes need. Please don't type names, phone numbers, addresses or anyone's health details into your story or chat.

2. Who processes your data for us

  • Google Firebase handles sign-in and stores your profile in a database that only your account can read.
  • Groq runs the AI model that reads your story and chat to create your skills, plan and answers. We never send your name or email. Groq states that it does not keep or train on these requests by default.
  • Himalayas receives only your job search words, sent from our server, to return remote job listings. It never receives your name, email, story or IP address.
  • Render hosts the website and keeps short server logs.

Some of these providers process data on servers outside India, such as in the USA. We don't sell your data, show ads, share your data with employers, or let anyone train AI models on it.

3. Your consent

We use your data only after you tick the consent box and sign in. You can withdraw consent at any time, as easily as you gave it: open More and choose Delete my account and data. This deletes your profile and account. Withdrawing consent doesn't affect anything we did before you withdrew it.

4. Your rights

  • Access: download everything we hold about you from More → Download my data.
  • Correction: edit your story and choices on any page. To update your name or photo, change them in your Google account.
  • Erasure: delete your account and data from More, or email us.
  • Grievance: email our contact below. We'll reply within 30 days, and always within the 90 days the law allows.
  • Nominate someone: email us to name a person who can use these rights for you if you die or can't act yourself.
  • Complain to the Data Protection Board of India if you're not satisfied after using our grievance process.

5. Children

ReBloom is only for people aged 18 or over. We don't knowingly process the data of anyone under 18. If you think a child has signed up, email us and we'll delete the account.

6. How long we keep your data

We keep your profile until you delete it. A guest profile is deleted when you end the guest session; if you leave without ending it, it stays until you delete it from More in the same browser or the project ends. If the ReBloom project ends, we'll delete all profiles. Server logs are kept by our hosting provider for a limited time, and for up to one year where Indian law requires it.

7. Security

The website uses HTTPS. Only signed-in users can use the AI and job search, and our API keys stay on the server. Database rules let each person read and change only her own profile. If a data breach affects you, we'll tell you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

8. Changes to this notice

If we change what data we use or why, we'll update this notice and ask for your consent again.

9. Contact

For questions, corrections, deletion, nominations or complaints, contact Team Phoenix Queens at the email shown here once it is set up.

Terms of Use

These terms apply when you use ReBloom. By ticking the box and signing in, you agree to them. Please also read our Privacy Notice.

1. What ReBloom is

ReBloom is a free student project by Team Phoenix Queens, built for the Sahyadri Science Talent Hunt 2026. It gives AI-made suggestions and shows job listings from other websites. It is not a recruitment agency or an employer, and it doesn't promise you a job or interview.

2. Who can use it

You must be 18 or older and either sign in with your own Google account or use guest mode. Keep your account secure and don't let others use it.

3. AI suggestions

Your skills profile, return plan, job match scores and Ask answers are made by an AI model and can be wrong or incomplete. They are general guidance, not legal, medical or financial advice. Check important details yourself, and for legal questions about maternity rights, confirm with your employer's HR or an official source.

4. Job listings

Listings come from Himalayas and the employers who post them. We don't check or endorse them. Filters such as work from home or flexible hours only look at what each listing says, so confirm the details with the employer. You apply on the listing's own website. Never pay anyone to get a job or an interview. Real employers don't ask for money. If a listing looks suspicious, don't share personal documents and report it to us.

5. Your content

Your story and choices stay yours. You allow us to process them only to provide ReBloom to you, as described in the Privacy Notice. Don't enter other people's personal details or anyone's health information.

6. Fair use

Don't misuse ReBloom: no attempts to break or overload it, get around its limits, copy its data in bulk, or use it for anything illegal or harmful. Because it runs on free services, each person has a limited number of AI requests per hour.

7. Availability and changes

ReBloom runs on free plans, so it may be slow to start, unavailable at times, or change. If we end the project, we'll delete all profiles. Where we can, we'll give notice first so you can download your data.

8. Responsibility

ReBloom is provided as it is, free of charge. As far as the law allows, Team Phoenix Queens is not responsible for decisions you make based on its suggestions or for third-party job listings. Nothing in these terms takes away rights you have under Indian law.

9. Ending your use

You can stop using ReBloom and delete your account at any time from More. We may suspend accounts that misuse the service.

10. Law

These terms are governed by the laws of India. Courts in Mangaluru, Karnataka have jurisdiction.
